CampusOS is the system which will be beneficial to college students in their college life. They can find whatever they need like anything about academics or personal
or skills-related. It will make college life better experience. 
Nowadays, students have to use multiple softwares like Google Classroom, Moodle, Discord, OLX, Notion and many more to keep track of all things and projects. So to 
make college life easier, CampusOS will integrate all these app features and also some more features in single system so that students don't have to manage all these
applications at a single time.
This platform is for all students and college faculties who have to manage all their daily college routines.
This solves the problem of managing all applications. Also students sometimes face difficulty of where to find what. Thsi problem is also solved due to AI interity
in CampusOS.
For future aspects, we don't want to limit CampusOS for only college students. It wil be also useful for school students and working professionals.
